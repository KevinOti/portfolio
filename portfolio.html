Automating backend FMCG workflows is what drives daily performance.

Operating directly within FMCG sales and commercial supply frameworks, I observed first-hand the massive operational bottleneck of manual record-keeping. Traditional logistics hubs generate heavy data fields—from daily dispatch matrices to mobile money ledgers—yet rely on manual clerical workflows to input them into ERP systems. To eliminate this friction, I developed specialized, backend automated data pipelines. By combining advanced Excel, relational database manipulation, and automated Python parsers, I convert hours of repetitive back-office formatting into safe, instant data pipelines.
